Shock

A missed payment is not one month

The fee lands on the highest APR. That debt gets no payment while interest still accrues. Extra sits out until the miss is over.

Worked example

A $1,000 balance at 12% with a $100 minimum finishes in 11 months and $58.98 of interest. Miss one month and add a $40 fee: 13 months and $75.37 of interest. Two months longer, $16.39 more interest, on a balance that looked small.

  • The late fee is added once, to the highest-APR open debt.
  • During the miss, that debt pays nothing. Other debts still pay their minimums.
  • Extra payments resume the month after the miss.

Calendar

Extra payments buy days, not just interest

People share a date. They come back to defend it. Framing the plan as days bought back is the line that gets pasted.

Worked example

A plan that finishes in 18 months, against 40 months of minimums, buys back 22 months. That is 670 days, about 96 weekends, and one birthday you do not spend sending interest.

  • Days use a 30.437-day month so a year stays a year.
  • Weekends are the day count divided by seven.
  • Birthdays are whole years inside the months saved.
  • Copy the line after you run the calculator — it is the share text.

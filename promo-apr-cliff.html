Promo timer

Kill the 0% balance before the cliff

A promotional APR is a deadline. Anything left on that card the month after the intro window is priced at the regular rate — often 20%+.

What the calculator does

If a debt has promo months set, the planner can aim extra principal at that balance first — even if avalanche would rather attack a higher everyday APR. It also solves the smallest extra that clears every promo balance before its reset month.

When this wins

  • You transferred a balance to a 0% card and the clock is under a year.
  • The regular APR after the promo is higher than your other cards.
  • You would rather pay a fee once than interest after the cliff.

When avalanche still wins

If the promo window is long and another card is already charging 28%, starving that card to hunt a distant cliff can cost more interest. Run both plans on your numbers.

Yearly accelerator

The 13th payment is one extra month, once a year

Mortgage forums treat it as folklore. On revolving debt it is just a scheduled snowflake equal to this month’s total outflow. The calculator prices it against your current extra so you can see the months and interest that disappear.

What it is

Take every minimum plus the extra you already send. Pay that same amount a thirteenth time each year. No new product. No refinance. Just one extra full payment, usually from a bonus, tax refund, or a month with no car insurance bill.

Why it works

  • The extra hits principal after interest has already been added for that month.
  • It compounds with snowball or avalanche extras you already run.
  • It is easier to commit to than raising the monthly extra forever.

Month-one split

Before you argue about strategy, look at this month: how many dollars of the payment reduce balance versus how many go to the bank. Drag the extra slider and watch the bank share shrink. That bar is the addiction loop—people stay to make the orange sliver smaller.

Versus biweekly

Twenty-six half-payments also create about one extra monthly payment a year, but they change cash-flow every payday. A 13th payment leaves the monthly budget alone and concentrates the hit. Run both cards if you want the screenshot for a partner conversation.

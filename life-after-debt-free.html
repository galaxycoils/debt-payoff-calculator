Keep the habit

The first year after debt-free

The payment already leaves your account every month. When creditors stop taking it, that cash can stay. Twelve months of that habit is the pile most people never screenshot.

The number is boring on purpose

Add every minimum plus the extra you already planned. That sum is what you “find” the month after the last payoff date. Multiply by 12. That is year one if lifestyle creep does not eat it.

A simple order of operations

  • Park one to three months of expenses if you skipped the emergency fund while attacking APR.
  • Match any workplace retirement dollar you were leaving on the table.
  • Then invest the same automatic amount that used to kill balances.

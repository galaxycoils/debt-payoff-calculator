Hybrid: snowball the first win, avalanche the rest

The fight is rarely “which method is theoretically cheapest.” It is whether you will still be sending extra after month four. Hybrid is the compromise people actually keep.

What hybrid means here

Sort by smallest balance first. The month that first debt hits zero, re-sort whatever is left by APR. Extra payment then behaves like avalanche until you are done.

Why it sticks

  • You still get a paid-off account early — the screenshot that keeps the streak alive.
  • You do not spend the next two years extra-paying a 7% loan while a 24% card compounds.
  • Interest usually lands closer to avalanche than to a full snowball run.

When not to bother

If every balance is similar size and APR, hybrid, snowball, and avalanche finish within a month of each other. If one tiny 0% promo sits next to a huge 29% card, skip the first-win theater and avalanche from day one.

The calculator card runs all three plans on the same debts so you can see the dollar gap before you pick a story to tell yourself.

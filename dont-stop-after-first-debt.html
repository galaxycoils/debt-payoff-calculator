Don't stop after the first debt

Snowball is built to give you a win early. That win is also the month people quietly go back to minimums.

The fade

After the smallest balance hits zero, the extra payment is supposed to roll onto the next debt. If it instead disappears into lifestyle, you keep only the remaining minimums. The calculator prices both paths on the same debts.

Worked example

A $200 card and a $1,000 card, both 0% APR, $50 minimums, $50 extra. Snowball kills the $200 first. Keep the extra and the second balance dies on the original schedule. Drop the extra after that first win and every leftover month is minimum-only.

Interest saved is rent you get back

A dollar of interest avoided is easier to share when it is a month of rent, a grocery week, or a flight home.

What the card computes

  • Interest avoided is the plan versus minimums only, same debts and strategy.
  • Rent is a live slider ($800–$4,000). Groceries stay at $150 a week. A flight stays at $400.
  • $2,400 avoided at $1,500 rent is 1.6 months of rent, 16 grocery weeks, and 6 flights.
  • Pin stores months and interest in this browser. The next drag that beats it gets a toast.

Why people stay

A gift month is a loan you give the lender

People drag the dip because December feels inevitable. The number that comes back is the reason they stay, copy the line, and come back in January.

Worked example

$2,400 at 0% with a $100 minimum and $100 extra finishes in 12 months. If the extra disappears in December (payment month 11 when you start in January), the plan slips to 13 months. The gifts did not cost a feeling. They cost a month.

  • The dip cannot exceed the extra. A zero extra month is the floor.
  • Other months keep the full extra, modeled as the reduced base plus snowflakes.
  • Pick any month — August travel costs the same shape of slip as December gifts.
  • Copy the line after you run the calculator. It is the share text.

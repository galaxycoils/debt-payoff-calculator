Calendar pressure

Debt-free by Christmas is a number, not a vibe

A debt-free date in “sometime 2028” does not move anyone. Christmas, tax day, and New Year do. The calculator binary-searches the smallest extra that lands each one.

Why these three dates

  • Christmas is the screenshot date. People want the card paid before they buy gifts on it.
  • Tax day is when a refund can become a snowflake — or when you want the balance gone first.
  • New Year is the resolution window. Arriving already free beats promising to start.

How the solver works

It takes your debts, current extra, and strategy, then asks: what extra would make payoff month ≤ months until that holiday? If you are already on track, the card says so. If not, one tap applies the solved extra to the live slider.

How to use the loop

Run the calculator after results load. Drag extra until a holiday flips to “On track.” That flip is the retention mechanic — people stay to manufacture a date they can text a partner.

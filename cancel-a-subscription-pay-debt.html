Cancel one subscription. Send it to debt.

A streaming bill feels tiny. Redirected every month it is a permanent extra payment — the same lever the calculator already uses.

Why this works

Interest compounds on the remaining balance. An extra $12 every month hits principal after minimums, so the next month’s interest is calculated on a smaller number. That is the same math as the live extra-payment slider.

Pick something you will not miss

  • $8–$16 unused streaming or delivery pass
  • A gym you have not visited this quarter
  • A duplicate music or cloud plan

Keep the subscriptions you use. The calculator is for the ones that already feel like waste.

Then lock it in

On the calculator, run your plan, tap a preset on the subscription card, and apply it to extra. Compare snowball vs avalanche again. The new debt-free date is the shareable number.

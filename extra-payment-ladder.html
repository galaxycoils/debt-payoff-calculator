What-if table

What does $25 more actually buy?

People stall on extras because $25 feels symbolic. The ladder puts six debt-free dates on one card so the next increment is a date, not a lecture.

How the ladder works

After you calculate, the tool reruns the same debts at $0 extra, your current extra, and four steps above it. Each row is a full amortization. Tap a row to put that extra on the live slider.

Partner chip-in

If someone else can add $50–$200 a month, the second slider stacks that amount on your extra and reports months saved plus interest avoided. Apply it when the screenshot is enough to start the conversation.

Why this keeps people on the page

  • Comparing six dates is faster than dragging the main slider blindly.
  • The “now” row anchors the table so the next step feels small.
  • Partner math is the shareable artifact for couples and roommates.

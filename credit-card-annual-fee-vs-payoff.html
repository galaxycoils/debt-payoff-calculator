Hidden leak

Keep the points card, or kill the fee first?

An annual fee is a scheduled snowflake in reverse. If the balance is still open on the anniversary, the issuer adds $95–$695 that then accrues interest like any other purchase.

How the calculator models it

Every 12th month, each debt that still has a balance is charged the fee you set. The fee is added to principal before that month’s interest. Debts that die before month 12 never pay it.

When the fee is noise

  • You finish in under a year.
  • The card’s credits (travel credit, lounge, insurance) cash-flow more than the fee this year.
  • You can product-change to a no-fee sibling and keep the credit line for utilization.

When the fee is the plan

A $695 premium card on a three-year payoff is two extra months for a lot of households. That is a screenshot people send a partner. Run the slider on your balances before you defend the lounge access.

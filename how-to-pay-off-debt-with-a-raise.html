Pay off debt with a raise

A raise that disappears into lifestyle does nothing to the payoff date. A raise that becomes extra payment compounds every month after it lands.

The lifestyle freeze

Keep spending at the pre-raise level for 12 months. Direct the take-home increase to extra debt payments the first payday it hits your account. Automate it so you never “decide” each month.

Why it works on long plans

If you already throw $100 extra at debt, a 3% raise on that extra is small in year one. Stacked yearly, it shortens multi-year plans by months and cuts interest on high-APR cards. The calculator’s raise slider models that stacking without a spreadsheet.

Don’t wait for a perfect raise

  • Side-hustle months can be snowflakes instead of a permanent raise.
  • If the raise is tiny, still route it. Habit beats size.
  • After the last debt dies, point the same payment at an emergency fund, then investments.

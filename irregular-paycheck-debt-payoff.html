Pay off debt on an irregular paycheck

Gig work, commissions, and two-paycheck months feel incompatible with a fixed extra payment. They are not. Split extras into a lean floor and a flush dump.

The lean floor

Pick an extra you can hit even on a thin month. That number is the plan’s floor. If a month is worse than the floor, skip extras (price that with the payment-holiday slider) rather than bouncing the whole plan.

The flush dump

Every second, third, or sixth month — overtime, a second paycheck, a tax refund — send a larger extra. The calculator treats those as scheduled snowflakes on top of the lean extra so the debt-free date moves without a spreadsheet.

Why this beats “I’ll catch up later”

  • Interest does not pause on lean months. A floor extra still cuts the balance.
  • Flush months without a written number get spent. Pre-commit the dump.
  • You can compare lean-only vs lean+flush after one calculate click.

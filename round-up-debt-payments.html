Round-up extras

If the total you already send is $347, send $350. The leftover $3 is small enough to ignore in the moment and large enough, stacked, to move a debt-free date.

Why round-ups stick

People abandon messy extras. They remember a clean $400. Rounding the whole outflow to the next $5, $10, $25, or $50 turns a number into a habit.

Worked example

A $1,000, 0% balance with a $100 minimum and $45 extra is $145 leaving the account. Rounding to the next $10 makes it $150. The calculator shows whether that $5 drops a month off the plan.

Rate cut, real fee

When refinancing beats staying

A 24% card rolled into an 8% loan looks like a win until the origination fee lands on the principal. Price stay vs refinance with the same extra payment.

What this compare actually does

  • Stay: current balances, APRs, mins, extra.
  • Refinance: one new balance = old principal + percent fee + flat fee, at the new APR, same extra.
  • Net saved = interest avoided minus the fee you paid up front.

If net is negative, the cut is marketing. Keep throwing extra at the original stack.

Where people get this wrong

They compare the new monthly payment to the old minimums and call the difference “savings.” That ignores term stretch. A 60-month loan can drop the payment and raise lifetime interest. The calculator keeps your extra so the calendar is honest.

Why this card holds people

Three live fields — new APR, fee percent, flat fee — invite tapping. The worth-it line is screenshot bait for a partner or a lender quote. That is time-on-site and a reason to reopen the tab when a mailer arrives.

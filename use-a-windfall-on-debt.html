Use a windfall on debt

A tax refund or bonus is a one-time snowflake. Sending it this month almost always beats dripping the same dollars later.

Why lumping wins

Interest accrues on whatever balance is still standing. A $600 bonus applied in month one stops a month of interest on $600. Split across six months and five of those months still charge you.

Worked example

A $1,000 balance at 0% with a $100 minimum takes 10 months. A $300 windfall this month cuts the plan to 7 months. Dripping $100 for three months also hits 7 months at 0% APR — add a real rate and the lump pulls ahead on interest.

Keep an emergency floor

If the bonus is the only cash cushion you have, park a small floor first, then send the rest. The calculator prices the debt side so you can see what that floor costs in months.

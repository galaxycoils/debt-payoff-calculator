Fuel

The snowball is a minimum that changes jobs

People stay on the page to watch the next kill feed the extra. The copy line is the month the extra doubles.

Worked example

A $200 store card and a $1,000 card, both at 0%, both with a $50 minimum, plus $50 extra. Snowball kills the store card in month 2. That $50 minimum joins the extra, so the extra doubles to $100. The card then dies in month 11.

  • Fuel is the minimum recorded on the kill, not a new payment you have to find.
  • The extra doubles when freed minimums catch the extra you already send.
  • Avalanche uses the same roll-in. Only the kill order changes.

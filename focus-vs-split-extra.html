Payment targeting

Should extra money hit one debt or all of them?

Fairness says split. Math and motivation usually say focus. The calculator runs both on the same extra so the argument ends with dates.

What “focus” means

Minimums stay on every account. The extra pile lands on one target — lowest balance (snowball) or highest APR (avalanche) — until that balance is gone. Then the extra walks to the next target.

What “split” means

The same extra is divided across every open debt each month. No account is ignored. First wins take longer because no single balance is being hunted.

Why focus usually wins

  • The first payoff frees that minimum, which becomes more extra.
  • Interest on the targeted balance stops earlier.
  • A date on the calendar is easier to screenshot than “everyone got $18.”

When split can look close

Equal balances, similar APRs, and a huge extra can finish in the same month. Even then, focus typically kills the first account sooner — the streak people actually feel.

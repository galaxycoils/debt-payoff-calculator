Balance transfer vs payoff

A 0% intro offer is not free. You pay a transfer fee up front, you get a promotional APR for a fixed number of months, then the leftover balance jumps to a regular rate. The only honest comparison is stay-put interest versus transfer interest plus the fee.

The three numbers that matter

  • Fee percent — usually 3–5% of the amount moved.
  • Promo APR and months — often 0% for 12–21 months.
  • Post-promo APR — the rate after the window.

When a transfer wins

It wins when interest avoided is larger than the fee and you can finish before the promo ends. Extra payments and annual snowflakes are how you force that finish line.

When staying put wins

If the fee is high, the promo is short, or you would finish soon anyway, the transfer just inflates the starting balance. If net-after-fee is negative, decline the offer.

How this calculator models it

Each transferred debt is increased by the fee. During promo months the engine uses the promo APR, then switches. The result card shows months saved, interest saved, fee paid, and net after fee.

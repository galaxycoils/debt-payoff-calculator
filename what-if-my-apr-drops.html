Rate daydream

A rate cut is the slider people will not stop dragging

APR shock shows the scare. This page is the fantasy: every rate falls, and the debt-free date jumps forward.

What the card actually computes

  • Same debts, same extra, same strategy — only APR drops by the slider amount.
  • Rates floor at 0%. A 4-point cut on a 1% loan does not go negative.
  • Autopay is the 0.25-point preset many cards advertise for enrolling.
  • Apply writes the new rates into the debt rows so snowball, charts, and kill order follow.

A worked card

A $2,400 balance at 24% with a $50 minimum and no extra takes 163 months and about $5,728 in interest. Cut 6 points to 18% and it is 86 months and about $1,875. That gap is the share line.

The 0.25 autopay discount on that same card is quieter: 153 months and about $485 saved. Small enough to ignore, large enough to screenshot.

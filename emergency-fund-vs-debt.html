Build a small cash cushion without abandoning extra payments.

A $1,000 starter fund stops a flat tire from becoming a new credit-card balance. Parking every extra dollar in savings while 24% APR compounds is expensive. Split the difference on purpose.

What the calculator measures

You already have an extra monthly payment. Divert some of it into savings at a chosen APY. The tool reruns the same snowball or avalanche math with the smaller extra, then grows the diverted cash month by month. You see months delayed, extra interest paid, and savings interest earned.

A useful default

  • Keep minimums + some extra on high-APR debt.
  • Park $50–$200/mo until you have $1,000 cash.
  • Then send the cushion amount back to extra.

On 0% promotional balances the delay is cheap. On 20%+ cards the interest usually beats a 4% HYSA — that is the “debt wins” flag on the results card.

Daily leaks are the other side of the same coin

$5 a day is about $152 a month. Redirecting a habit you already dislike is often easier than cutting a bill you use. The calculator turns that daily number into extra principal and a new debt-free date.

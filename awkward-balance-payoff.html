Cliff

The awkward leftover is the debt that will not die

A balance of $250 with a $50 minimum takes five months. Paying the $50 cliff to $200 in month one finishes in four. That is the screenshot.

Worked example

The cliff is the gap from the smallest open balance down to the next $100 or $500. If the balance is already on the step, the cliff is one full step. Dropping it in month one is a snowflake, not a new lifestyle.

  • $250 to the next $100 is a $50 cliff and one month saved at 0% with a $50 minimum and no extra.
  • $200 already on a hundred drops a full step, to $100.
  • The button writes that amount into month 1 so the schedule updates.
  • People return to knock the next cliff after the first one lands.

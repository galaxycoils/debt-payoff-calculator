What waiting costs

“I’ll start extras after the holidays” is a plan. It also has a price. Every month of minimums-only lets high APR keep compounding on the full balance.

The delay is not a pause. Interest does not pause.

If you plan to throw $200 extra at a 24% card, waiting three months does not save you $600 of cash. You still spend those three minimums. You also let three months of interest stick to the principal the extra would have killed.

A clean way to price it

  • Run the plan starting this month.
  • Run the same extra, but extras begin in month 2, 4, or 7.
  • The gap is extra interest plus extra months to debt-free.

On 0% promotional balances the dollar gap can be tiny and the calendar gap is closer to the wait itself. On double-digit APR the dollar gap is the screenshot people send a partner.

Why this card keeps people on the page

Toggling 1 / 2 / 3 / 6 months is a live slider for regret. The coffee-count framing makes the interest feel spendable. Both raise time-on-site and the chance someone hits Calculate again after a paycheck.
